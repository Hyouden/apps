Fastest way to save from social media
  1. In Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, tap Share → Copy link.
  2. Open Recipe Box and tap Add recipe → Paste copied link.
  3. Name it, tap a tag or two, save.
On AndroidOnce Recipe Box is installed from Chrome, it shows up right in the Share menu: tap Share → Recipe Box.
