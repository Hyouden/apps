How to delete these on your phone

iPhone: open Photos, tap Select, tap each photo shown here, then the trash icon. Then go to Albums → Recently Deleted → Delete All to free the space.

Android: open Google Photos, long-press to select these, tap Delete, then empty Trash under Library.

Tip: keep this screen open in a split view or take a screenshot so you can match them up.
