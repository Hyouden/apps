      How the numbers work

      Two decks are compared copy by copy. Three copies of a card in one deck and two in the other count as two shared out of three. 100% means the same 40 cards; 0% means none in common. The Legend, runes and battlefields are left out of the score.

      Archetypes are decks that are, on average, at least as alike as the "Group at" setting. Core cards are in every deck of a group; flex cards are in only some.

      Where the data comes from

      Tournament lists are the ones Riot publishes on playriftbound.com after each major event. "Best-of" lists are the best-finishing pilot of each Legend. Deck codes use the open format from Piltover Archive. Card names come from the riftbound-tools dataset, and updates from RiftHunt.
